# LectureLens: 2-minute demo

A clean path through the Ask-first prototype using three questions: one in scope, one from another week, and one outside the course. The times are a guide for a 2-minute slot.

## Before you start (30 seconds, off the clock)

1. Open the app in a **fresh private/incognito window**, so there are no saved items or tips from earlier runs.
   - Or, in any window: **My notes → Clear my data → Yes, clear my data**.
2. Zoom the browser to 100%. The type is already sized for a projector.
3. Optional: open a second tab with `?debug=1` on the same URL for the metrics at the end. It shares the same log.
4. Each question can be typed, or pasted from this file:
   - `is it too late to start saving at 35?`
   - `how do I raise my credit score?`
   - `should I buy bitcoin?`

## The path

### 0:00 · Landing (10 s)
- **Say:** “Students don’t review a lecture from the top. They review from the thing that confused them. So the first screen is just one question.”
- **Point at:**
  - the headline *What are you stuck on?*;
  - the **Campus AI · runs on university servers** badge;
  - the line saying answers come only from this course.

### 0:10 · Question 1, in scope (50 s)
1. Type **`is it too late to start saving at 35?`** and press **Enter**.
   - *Finding the moment…* shows briefly, then the answer appears.
2. **Say:** “It didn’t write an essay. It found the moment in the lecture: Week 6, 31:30–37:40, *Ava vs Ben: the cost of waiting*.”
3. Press **Play**.
   - Captions run in sync with the transcript.
   - The scrubber shows the clip as a teal band.
   - A first-run tip above the player disappears once you press Play.
   - Pause after a few seconds.
4. In **Quick recap**, click the **Slide 7** chip.
   - The evidence drawer slides in with the exact slide.
   - Click **Read what was said** to see the transcript around that moment, then press **Esc** to close it.
   - **Say:** “Every line of the recap is traceable to a slide or a timestamp.”
5. Click **Explain differently → With numbers**. Ava pays in $20,000 and ends with $225,073; Ben pays in $60,000 and ends with $202,146.
6. Click **Still stuck**.
   - **Say:** “If it didn’t land, the student says so, and it’s saved for later with a simpler explanation already open.”

### 1:00 · Question 2, another week (25 s)
1. Scroll to **Still have a question?**, type **`how do I raise my credit score?`**, then press **Enter**.
2. The answer reads *That’s covered in Week 4*. It plays the Week 4 moment, *The five parts of a credit score* (6:20–12:45), rather than forcing an answer from Week 6.
3. **Say:** “It knows the whole syllabus, so it sends you to the right week instead of guessing.”

### 1:25 · Question 3, outside the course (20 s)
1. Type **`should I buy bitcoin?`** and press **Enter**.
2. The answer reads *This course doesn’t cover cryptocurrency* and points to the closest material: Week 7 on diversification and risk.
3. **Say:** “It won’t make things up. Out-of-scope questions get an honest answer and a pointer to a human.”

### 1:45 · Close the loop (15 s)
1. Click the **LectureLens** logo to go home.
   - The landing now shows **Still stuck on 1 thing**.
   - Click it to reopen the answer at the time you left it, with *Simpler* open.
2. Optional, if there's time:
   - **My notes** shows the sample note and the **AI may use my notes** switch.
   - Turning it off removes the **My note** chips from recaps.
3. Optional, for the success signals: open the `?debug=1` tab. It shows:
   - questions asked;
   - time to answer;
   - time to first useful action;
   - % of answers where the moment was played;
   - % marked Got it or Still stuck;
   - source chips opened.

## Reset after the demo
- **My notes → Clear my data**. This clears Still stuck, settings, tips and the usage log.
- Or close the incognito window.

## If something goes off-script
- **A typo or odd phrasing:** the matcher tolerates typos. If nothing matches, it says *I couldn’t find that in this course* and offers questions it can answer. That's a good moment to show too.
- **An empty question:** the box explains what to type. It doesn't fail silently.
- **Back to the start:** the **LectureLens** logo always returns home and keeps the Still stuck list.
